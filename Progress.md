## Phase 7 — Sleep score matches Apple (S8)
- ✅ Sleep score rebuilt to match Apple's Sleep Score (2026-11-09) — `SleepScoring` = fitted model (duration kink at `durationReferenceMinutes` 420, asymmetric bedtime vs mean onset, interruptions n + minutes beyond 5/wake-up), whole-minute inputs, round half up then clamp; every constant in `SleepScoreConfig`. Measurement (`SleepSession`): union of asleep samples from every source on the clock-minute grid (each minute a session touches is asleep or awake, whichever covers more; gaps are neither); sessions split by gaps > 2 h; sleep day D = sessions ending in [D−1 18:00, D 18:00) (night + same-day naps); onset = start of the longest session; wake-ups = runs of awake minutes inside the main sleep; average = mean onset over the 13 previous sleep days with a real night (≥ 1 night, else 30). `SleepScore.night` (`SleepNight`) carries the inputs; `SleepScoring.scores(forSleepDays:)` batch used by Week. Today/widget snapshot/reminders/goal go through `HealthSync.refresh` → same path. Tests pin the formula and its breakpoints at synthetic inputs plus measurement edge cases
- ✅ S7b: nap-only days show — (2026-11-09) — a sleep day whose main sleep is < `minMainSleepForBedtimeHistory` (3 h) returns nil from `SleepScoring.score`/`scores`, so Today, Week, widget snapshot, sleep goal, streaks and reminders treat it like a no-data day ("—", Watch hint). Naps on a day with a real night still count; nap-only days are skipped in the bedtime average. Tests: nap-only → nil (single + batch), 3 h threshold boundary

- 2026-11-08: **Device bug: widget (+) did nothing** (lock + home screen, no press feedback). Root cause: iOS 26's App Intents indexer (`linkd`) rejected Fuely's whole App Intents metadata. `LogWaterIntent` lived in the FuelyCore Swift package and reached the app/widget through `AppIntentsPackage` includes; linkd on iOS 26.x logs ``metadata `_$s9FuelyCore0aB10AppIntentsV' did not match any imported symbol`` (FuelyCore is linked statically) and then `aggregateMetadataIsEmpty`, so no intent (incl. the Circle configuration) was registered. Reproduced on a fresh iOS 26.5 simulator (the test phone runs iOS 26, not 27); the 27.1 runtime accepts it, which is why the earlier "26.5 sim linkd" note looked like a simulator quirk. Fix: the `AppIntent` struct moved to `FuelyWidgets/Shared/LogWaterIntent.swift` with target membership in both app and widget (synchronized-folder exception in the pbxproj); its logic is `LogWaterAction.perform(amountML:)` in FuelyCore; the `AppIntentsPackage` types were removed. linkd on 26.5 now registers Fuely with no errors; tests green; reinstalled on the test iPhone. Open: on device the App Group container (`devicectl … appGroupDataContainer`) showed no `Fuely/` store files, to be checked once the user confirms whether the widgets show water logged in the app.
